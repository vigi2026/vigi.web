About the Project

Smart Security &
Student Productivity

VIGI LOCK is a proposed Android application that merges cybersecurity awareness with academic productivity tools — helping BCA students stay digitally safe while managing their studies effectively.

The project addresses two major challenges faced by modern college students: rising cyber threats targeting inexperienced mobile users, and the lack of structured, intelligent tools for managing daily academic schedules and revision cycles.

Core Features

Everything You Need in
One Intelligent App

VIGI LOCK combines two powerful pillars — cyber protection and academic management.

🔗
URL & Link Analyzer

Heuristic validation to check URL format, suspicious keyword patterns, protocol security, and domain inconsistencies before you click.

CYBERSECURITY
📁
File Safety Analyzer

Inspects proposed APK permissions, generates SHA-256 hashes, and evaluates uploaded documents for suspicious metadata patterns.

CYBERSECURITY
🎙️
Voice Assistant

Hands-free interaction using Android SpeechRecognizer and TextToSpeech — ask VIGI about your schedule or security status by voice.

AI & VOICE
📅
Study Timetable

Create and manage structured daily academic schedules with subject hours, revision slots, and practical programming practice sessions.

PRODUCTIVITY
⏰
Smart Reminders

AlarmManager and WorkManager-backed notifications for deadlines and study slots, guaranteed to fire even when the app is backgrounded.

PRODUCTIVITY
📊
Progress Tracker

Monitor study session completion, revision consistency, and daily academic goals with intuitive visual progress indicators.

ANALYTICS
🧠
Phishing Awareness

Educational tips and red-flag checklists that teach users to recognize social engineering, urgent fake messages, and spoofed domains.

EDUCATION
🔒
On-Device Privacy

All timetable data and scan preferences stored securely using Room & SQLite — no private data sent to external servers without consent.

PRIVACY
💡
Plain-English Explanations

Translates cryptic SSL errors and security warnings into clear, actionable guidance that any student can understand and act on immediately.
